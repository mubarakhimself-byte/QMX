Label the sampled items the way you would label any item of this kind.

The brief lists `sampled`: items a script also labelled, sent to you so its labels keep being checked
against yours. Reply with one JSON document: `labels`, one entry per sampled item, each
`{"item_id", "label"}`. The labels are `method`, `data`, `reference` and `other`. Label from the
title alone; when the title carries nothing you can label, say `other`.

You change the search space of QMX's overnight Lab loop after a plateau. The input is tonight's state: the search space, the trials so far with their gates, and the moves already made.

Pick exactly one `move` from the fixed list: `add_class` (add a signal class, filter or exit), `drop_class` (drop one that never survives), `widen_range` or `narrow_range` (one parameter range), or `switch_timeframe` (to another timeframe inside the markets in scope). Give its `target` (`signal_classes`, `filters` or `exits` for a class move, the parameter's name for a range, `timeframes` for a switch), its `value` (the class, the new low and high as a pair, or the timeframe), and the `reason` in one sentence from the trials. Changing the fitness function or a gate threshold is not a move.

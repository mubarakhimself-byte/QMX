## Durable completion, restart, reuse and cancellation

**Fixed total versus additional generations is a required adapter distinction.** `run_alternative_study` reconstructs persisted history, resumes its fresh stepper, then passes `generation_count` to `run_study_generations`, which performs that many **additional** generations. For a descriptor declaring total N, compute remaining work from checked history. At N complete generations, perform readback-only completion/reuse; do not call the driver again with N. Refuse future/out-of-range generation or ask coordinates. A partial generation re-proposes the same batch, skips its completed asks and conditions only after the entire barrier is durable.

`StudyGenerationRun.fp1_identity()` describes only the generations returned by that invocation. Therefore a restart's incremental driver result must not become the canonical whole-Study completion identity. Reconstruct all ordered planned history from the terminal store so uninterrupted execution, partial restart and readback reuse identify the same complete domain artifact. Extending the target budget changes the full Study declaration/identity; compatible carry-forward needs explicit successor/reuse semantics, not silent history adoption.

Existing Study code reserves trial intent before evaluation and can reconstruct an interrupted trial from its pinned original CT-13 writer before appending the terminal. The application adapter must preserve this: never interpret an incomplete reservation as proof evaluation did not occur, never discard decisions and blindly evaluate twice, and never condition on partial results. Check original candidate/source/split, initial accounting state, interpreter, writer/epoch and registered lineage on reconstruction. Stable evidence writer/epoch belongs to the domain identity; generating a new writer from every QMA job/attempt would defeat normal reuse. Every reused terminal must still be checked against the exact declared Study and current allowed invocation context.

**Producer occurrence:** after the complete Study artifact and all cited trial evidence have been reopened, the adapter writes and reopens a private CT-13 occurrence binding the exact descriptor/operator binding, Study/completion fingerprint, source frontier, full current job/attempt/spec/evidence/split/environment/occupancy and pinned writers/epoch. Both claimed and claimless verification require that occurrence and the complete artifact; a lone trial or complete domain artifact without the producer occurrence does not prove that invocation. Reuse the private occurrence mechanism without creating a new public journal kind. A Study occurrence may use the Alternative domain discriminator and a binding/completion fingerprint that carries the full Study identity.

Same-invocation restart/readback may reuse its existing valid occurrence idempotently. A different job/attempt must not acquire success by relabeling old trial/result bytes. If cross-invocation content reuse is intended, define it explicitly: the adapter executes a real checked reuse path and produces its own current invocation occurrence over the unchanged complete domain artifact; the result states reuse rather than claiming new trial evaluation. Otherwise refuse missing/foreign occurrence. This policy must be fixed before its normal-reuse positive is accepted.

Cancellation is checked at the Study adapter's trial boundaries using QMA's existing child cancellation signal/path. QMB records the aborted/partial invocation in its own ATC evidence; QMA must not write that ledger. Completed trials and pending intents remain durable, but no complete Study artifact/producer completion witness is minted for an incomplete target. Preserve the service's serialized child-exit/cancel handling so a cancelled JobHandle cannot later become DONE from a racing verifier. Forced termination or uncertain observation requires the existing explicit certainty/refusal handling, not fabricated success. Reentry reads the partial history/reservation and obeys the barrier; it does not mutate a terminal cancelled handle or automatically resolve UNKNOWN. Missing-witness or persistence failures remain visible.

## QMA expected artifact and JobHandle integration

Current `CliQmbDoorTransport` preflight validation admits only Book/Alternative modes; its terminal parser admits exactly `qmb-recorded-replay-result-v1` or `qmb-alternative-result-v1`. `BacktestingService` maps every false CT-32 mode to the latter and requires single-run `run_receipt_fp1`, `terminal_artifact_fp1` and final-accounting fields. A Study cannot satisfy that by returning its last/best trial.

Add the private Study parser/mapping at preparation, durable expected-context reconstruction, claim parsing, verified terminal parsing and complete-proof validation. Preserve exact context types and all identity comparisons. QMA carries refs and the trusted expected class/descriptor; QMB owns Study/domain interpretation. At placement, the existing CT-11 expected-context artifact is PARTIAL. DONE requires an observed successful actual child exit, reopened complete Study result plus producer occurrence, immutable verified-context proof linked to that expected artifact/digest, and an attached COMPLETE `JobArtifact`. A missing/partial/mismatched result or proof-write failure must retain uncertainty/refusal rather than becoming DONE. Existing explicit operator UNKNOWN resolution remains separate and must cite the reopened complete proof; claimless recovery does not create completion evidence or invoke evaluators.

No new QMA JobHandle state, registry kind, event bus or daemon-owned Study history is needed. Normal registered card/graph/outbox publication and actual QMA operation execution remain required proof after implementation; direct adapter calls, placeholder preflight, or a local CLI fixture do not supply it.

# Harness requests

The engine uses only the names exported by `qma/harness/__init__.py` on `qma/hp17` [retrieved]. The account pool is the one sanctioned extension (ruling 39) and is designed in `SOLUTION-DESIGN.md` section 7, not requested here. Everything below is a request for the operator to grant or refuse. **Every row has a fallback that needs no harness edit**, so none blocks the first build; the fallbacks cost quality, speed or tidiness, as each row says.

**H0, H15, H16 and H17 were granted on 2026-10-17 (ruling 89) and built as HG1 (#92).** They needed his explicit grant (27b B5, ruling 88): they are outside ruling 39's load-balancer exception, and ruling 24 says the harness is not touched. They are the top call in OPEN-DECISIONS (A). H0 and H17 are export-only and table-row edits; H15 and H16 add per-chat options to `NewChat` for Background and Child chats only.

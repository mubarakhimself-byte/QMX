5. **What counts as an irreversible external effect.** The only steps that may wait for the inbox. Recommend: messages, posts, webhooks, outbound calls to non-QMX systems, desktop actions that submit or send, and app operations an app marks so; model spend, Library writes and file changes never count.
6. **Default self-improvement rule.** Recommend `self_improve: auto` for workflows he created or enabled, `propose_only` for templates he has not touched; `main` moves only by his click.

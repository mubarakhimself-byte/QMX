# Units: the harness in nine PR-sized packets

Each unit lands on `rebuild/base` through the orchestrator, with its "done when" shown in the temporary console (`python -m qma2.console`) and a live check the orchestrator runs at the head SHA. "Live" means a real model or a real OS facility, never a fake client. Fakes are allowed in unit tests underneath, not as the gate.

Lanes: U0 → U1 → U2 → {U3 ∥ U4 ∥ U8} → {U5 ∥ U6} → U7. Three builders at most at once (operator ruling on Max 5x).

## What the first unit proves

U1 is the first thing that has ever run a Claude turn on the operator's subscription from QMX. Its live check is deliberately small (a read-only turn, no gate) so that a failure points at one of three things: sign-in in the QMX home, the SDK pin on Windows, or the event mapping. Everything after U1 assumes those three are settled.

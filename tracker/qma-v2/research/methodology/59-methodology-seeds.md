## Explainers inside QMX (operator, 2026-10-24)
- The copilot and workflows should explain things visually, not in text walls. Two free tools to build this on:
  - **anatomy** (https://github.com/wheresryan22/anatomy): an agent skill that draws systems as detailed, interactive isometric SVG "machines" with labelled parts, optional WebGL. Fits system maps, workflow and mini-app diagrams, data journeys.
  - **Manim** (the 3Blue1Brown library): programmatic animated videos. Fits maths and strategy explanations (risk, sizing, distributions, backtest results).
- Where they could fit: copilot answers rendered as explainers in chat; a skill or toolset the copilot calls to "explain this visually"; workflow and Lab outputs as animated reports; Library items getting an explainer view. Decide in the methodology session; check licences and that both stay free.

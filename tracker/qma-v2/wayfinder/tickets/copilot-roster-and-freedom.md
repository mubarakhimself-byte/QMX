## Question

The organogram's people: one hub copilot as the place he talks, the starter sub-agent roles, the thin menus that forward to shared workflows, and the review panels as lenses. Roles and reviewers are configurable, never hard-coded.

## Why it matters

Corrections F and H replace "modes" with capability packs and forbid a fixed roster. BMad's agent-first warning stands: one hub with routed capabilities beats many single-purpose agents. The hub must route by scanning artifacts against the capability graph, not by remembering.

## Recommended answer

One hub. Starter sub-agent roles (editable through the builder): researcher, builder, tester, validator, librarian, scanner, explainer, each an agent folder. Menus: analyst, quant, validator, risk officer, librarian, toolsmith, each forwarding to a template or a role. Review panels: a strategy review crew (overfit hunter, leak hunter, regime skeptic, cost realist, pragmatist) and an anti-consensus club for go/no-go, run as independent agents on the families Settings names. Trader agents are autonomous workflows, not copilots. No mini app has its own copilot.

## What the session does

Grilling on the names he wants and on which menus matter first. Output: the roster table (name, folder, default role mapping, home) and the panel list, all as editable data.

## Unblocks

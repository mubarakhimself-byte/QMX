## Question

Rules like "a run may not skip a check" bind agents only. You can drop any check in your own workflows, and the result shows a badge. Agree?

## Recommended answer

Yes.

## What it unblocks

Total creative freedom in the Lab: cheap sanity checks only on Board runs, the full gates only when something leaves the Lab, his own tweaks not counted as trials, the "check removed" badge. The gate store (C8) and the trial ledger (C9) are written to this rule.

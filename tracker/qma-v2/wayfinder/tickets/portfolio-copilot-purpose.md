## Question

What the Portfolio pack does now: a human-driven watcher with scripts and templates, where a combined session may propose a Portfolio change from Lab or app output with his approval, while every sizing question parks behind the risk session.

## Why it matters

Corrections 6, A and G. Portfolio is always human-driven and may shrink under the self-configuring unit. The unit lives inside the trading node, a server you upload a unit to (premature; out of this map). Today Portfolio has broker reads and unsigned proposals only (`05` §5).

## Recommended answer

Now: the morning brief and the portfolio watchdog templates (scripts), read-only simulations (correlation, rebalance frequency, sizing variants as scripts), decision cards with recommendation and default, journal summary, the book picture. A combined session may turn Lab or app output into a Portfolio proposal card; approval writes it. Never: money, limits, kill switch, the account guard (locked by its own ticket). Parked behind the risk session: allocation, sizing suggestions, the meaning of pod and unit, paper per unit, where the router lives.

## What the session does

Grilling on what he wants to see every morning and on the proposal card. Output: the Portfolio pack's menu and read-model needs, with the parked list.

## Unblocks

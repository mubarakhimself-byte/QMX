## The main problem: the first game plan

- **$500 to $5,000 in a month.** My earlier reply said it is possible but needs a large risk per trade. His frame: don't drop the goal because of that. Ask "how do we achieve this without getting ruined?" Accelerators and brakes, including reverse house of money, exist for this.
- **Cents per trade are out for this goal.** A unit risking under $1 or $2 isn't realistic to him. His logic: spreading tiny bets across many units is a later luxury. First build a cash base. "At 5,000, the math changes drastically."
- **Not wrong, wrong fit.** He said the many-units sizing "is not wrong per se, it's just not the right fit" at this capital level.
- **Game theory and concentration.** With small capital, why widen the blast radius to all instruments? Find the best basket and the best fit for the unit, and position it to win. For example, leave crypto out at first.
- **Where each unit goes.** Which instrument, market, session and sniping window suits it. This includes unit placement as a kind of position sizing.
- **More units means less per unit.** His complaint: a system that trades all day gets very little to trade with.

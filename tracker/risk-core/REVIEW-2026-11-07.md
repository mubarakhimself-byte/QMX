# 7 Nov: the template missed his ask

He read the explainer and rejected it (M49, `seeds/operator-words-2026-11-06.md`). An independent transcript audit and a direct look at the UI, the UI notes and the code agree with him.

## His ask, restated

Technical lens only: a unit that can trade and size itself, on a baseline template with a customization layer, usable by someone new to QMX. Numbers, brakes policy and validation methodology come after, and are argued separately. Agents and mini apps carry monitoring and extension. He also expected to be taken through what the papers found.

Compaction did not lose it. The deliverable sentence stayed in STATE.md. The work stopped obeying it.

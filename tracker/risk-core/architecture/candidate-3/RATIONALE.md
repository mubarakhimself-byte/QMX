# Candidate 3: what was weighed and what was rejected

Fable, 6 Nov 2026. Under a page. The spine records decisions; this records the roads not taken.

**Where the unit's brakes live.** The assignment was F-B (b): halt slots in the unit. I took it seriously rather than minimally: the unit's bench, level, CUSUM and cohorts all live in the compiled unit, not only the bench. Rejected: a half-way shape where the bench is a unit halt but the level is a guard number. It would have split one unit's self-judgement across two owners and made "the unit stands alone" a slogan. The cost accepted: a cross-unit brake that needs a new fact costs a kernel PR; the needs list makes that loud.

**One arbiter against two.** Rejected: a single rank table applied in the guard with the unit's halts reported up as proposals. That is (a) in disguise. Kept two stages and proved they equal one table because both are cut-only; the property test is the guarantee, not a convention. The raise is the only non-cut, so it was made a fact (`accelerator_factor`) the guard sets only when every brake is clear, never a guard answer.

**Fork F-A.** Chose refresh at anchors. Rejected the applier with modes for this shape: its herding cap is cross-unit and would pull number motion back into the guard; `at_flat` mid-day motion is not something he asked for; the one strong case for it (costs after a broker change) is answered by treating costs as facts. If the harness shows a one-anchor lag costs money on X4-60, (a) wins and the document is the same.

**Facts as a package of their own.** Rejected: facts computed inside the guard and handed to units through it. That makes the harness and the unit depend on the guard's adapters. A named kernel with two adapters lets the unit, the guard and the harness read one thing, and the conformance test pins it.

**Where exits rank.** Chose the compiler's fixed order inside the unit (close beats partial beats amend; earlier binding wins ties). Rejected the guard's safest-wins: exits never reach the guard, and a configurable exit rank is a knob nobody asked for.

**Account-scope halts in the unit document.** Rejected keeping them: a unit cannot judge the account. They refuse with a message pointing at the portfolio config. The field stays in the model for the census test.

**The drift-to-paper rule.** The proof script found a 42 % paper rate on healthy units at h = 17 and a 500-trade window. Rejected changing the rule here: it is a number, and the numbers lens owns it. Made it a derivation on the drift row so it moves without code, and said what I think the fix is.

**Cut-only agent path as a fact.** Rejected a proposal queue inside the guard at go-live (X1-H50's full machinery): no agent job exists yet. A cut with a TTL as one sizer input is enough and is the same seam the press and the taper use.

**Not chosen, kept for the fold.** The cluster level (Q8), stacked packs, the trust ladder, step 3 exits, multi-position units. Each has a plug point named in the spine's Deferred list.

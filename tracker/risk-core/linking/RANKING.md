# How ideas are ranked

**Who ranks.** The Fable funnel judge ranks, after Stage B. An Opus reviewer then attacks the ranking. Expansion and linking agents do not rank. They may tag their own ideas solid, plausible or wild, and that tag is only a hint.

**The aim.** The operator wants performance with intention, not just safety (M33). Survival is a constraint the design must meet; it is not the thing to maximise. A design that never touches the floor but grows nothing is a failure: the "sitting duck".

## Scores per idea, 0 to 3 each

1. **Performance.** How much the idea can add to growth after costs: more good bets, better size on good bets, less waste. Name the mechanism.
2. **Survival.** Whether the floor stays rare and losses stay bounded. Test it when the edge is weaker than believed, when units lose together, and on gaps and news. A 0 here blocks the idea, whatever its other scores.
3. **Evidence.** What backs the idea:
   - 3: live or real-money results;
   - 2: a fair-controlled test (for brakes, beating a random brake that fires as often);
   - 1: the author's own backtest;
   - 0: an idea only.
4. **Asymmetry.** Payoff against cost: small to build or run, big upside, small downside if wrong. Count the ledger variables it adds as cost.
5. **Fit.**
   - Does it work across instrument classes, styles and timeframes?
   - Does it plug into the shipped stack, or justify replacing a part of it (M31)?
   - Does it respect the hard lines? Agents never touch money. Only he promotes. Exits are never blocked. The kill switch only goes down by his hand.
6. **Self-configuring.** Can its numbers derive themselves with a stated reason, and can he change how they derive, not only their values?

## What the judge produces

For every decision point:
- **The pick.** One pick; two picks when they serve different styles or classes; or a range.
- **The reason** for each pick.
- **What changed.** The collisions it resolved and the ideas it dropped, each with why.

## What is not ranked here

Some ideas are trading signals or market readings rather than risk and sizing, for example multi-level order flow imbalance for scalping. These go to `linking/IDEA-BANK.md` as unit or reading seeds. Each seed records:
- the source;
- the signal;
- the style and instruments it suits;
- the data QMX would need.

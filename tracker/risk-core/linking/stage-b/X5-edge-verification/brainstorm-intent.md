## The few directions that matter

1. **Three speeds, one evidence currency.** Inputs (costs, fills, trade rate) act in days; e-detector warnings in weeks; a funded e-process confirms "the edge is below its minimum viable line" over hundreds of trades. A money brake sits apart and never waits for statistics.
2. **Risk-tiered thresholds by size level.** Proof continues at the smallest live size, and the evidence bar rises with the level (e-value 10 at paper, 20 at level 1, 50 at level 2, 100 at level 3). The ladder spends trades before it spends money.
3. **"Ten times faster" needs a correlation column.** Ten units at correlation 0.3 count as 2.7; the plan must show N_eff beside N, and an event window so one shared move counts once.
4. **Costs are the master input.** A 0.05R cost rise is visible at 8.8 standard errors after 50 trades; a 0.05R edge drop needs about 1,900 trades to confirm. The machine check is honest in a week; edge proof is not.
5. **Every gate must beat a random control**, and the whole pipeline is tested on null paths and injected edges with its delay reported. Otherwise the gates are decoration.
6. **Approval binds to the exact action.** His click approves a hash, a market list, a level and limits; it expires; any edit is a new candidate.

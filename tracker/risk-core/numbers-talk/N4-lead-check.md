# N4: the lead's check

2026-11-09. Two checks on N4 before relaying it.

1. **E-nano futures exist.** Confirmed on CME's pages: listed 24 August 2026. NES $0.50, NNQ $0.20, N2K $0.50 and NDOW $0.05 per point, trading 23 hours a day.
2. **E-nano fees were missing from N4, and they decide the case.** Exchange fee about $0.35 per side (Topstep help, SNIPPET; a member rate of $0.31 is reported). Broker commission about $0.25-0.29 per side. NFA $0.01 per side. That is about $1.22 a round trip plus one tick of spread. All of these are fixed per contract, so they weigh ten times more on a nano than on a micro.

ESTIMATE: uses N3's median 5-minute moves (US100 42.4 points, US500 5.84 points, US30 52.5 points) and assumes one tick of spread.

Reading: at $500, index CFDs are the cheaper way to place a bet. Futures fees are fixed per contract, so they only beat CFDs at micro size (MES, MNQ), which suits roughly a $5,000 account. Futures data ($12.10 a month per CME exchange) is still worth buying as a signal. Exchange fees should be confirmed on CME's fee schedule.

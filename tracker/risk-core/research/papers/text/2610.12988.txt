Abstract
Cryptocurrency swing trading spans mismatched timescales:
positionsremainactivewhilemarketandeventriskcanchange
within minutes. However, many predictive and large lan-
guage model (LLM)-based trading systems assess risk af-
ter strategy generation or run both functions at the same ca-
dence, leaving proposals unconditioned by current risk and
exposure weakly governed between revisions. We propose
SwingGuard, a dual-timescale, long-only multi-agent frame-
work for risk-conditioned strategy generation. At the strat-
egy timescale, specialized analysts synthesize point-in-time
technical, sentiment, on-chain, and macroeconomic evidence
through bullish–bearish deliberation. The resulting evidence
and current risk context are then combined by a Portfolio Ar-
chitecttoformatargetposition.Atthegovernancetimescale,
afive-minuteRiskControllerperformsconditionaldiagnosis,
maintains persistent risk states with staged recovery, audits
the proposal, and constrains execution until the next strategy
revision. This design allows risk to shape the feasible strat-
egy before proposal finalization while continuing to govern
the active position afterward. Evaluations on Bitcoin (BTC)
andEther(ETH)showthatSwingGuardpreservespositivere-
turns after transaction costs while maintaining shallow draw-
downs and strong risk-adjusted performance on both assets.
Theexposure-cappedjointportfolioachievesaSharperatioof
2.98, compared with 2.79 for an otherwise identical Post-hoc
Risk variant. Structured traces further link risk evidence and
state transitions to strategy constraints and executed position
changes, providing an auditable framework for asynchronous
cryptocurrencyswingtrading.Overall,theresultsindicatethat
conditioningstrategyformationoncurrentrisk,togetherwith
persistenthigh-frequencygovernance,improvesrisk-adjusted
performance without sacrificing auditability.

Related Work
Cryptocurrency forecasting and reinforcement learning es-
timate returns or map signals to positions (Zhang, Cai, and
Wen 2024; John, Binnewies, and Stantic 2024; Jiang and
Liang 2017; Liu et al. 2023b; Jing and Kang 2024), while
technical, news, and momentum evidence remains regime
sensitive (Hudson and Urquhart 2021; Anamika and Subra-
maniam 2022; Asness, Moskowitz, and Pedersen 2013; Ca-
kici et al. 2024). Volatility-managed and regime-switching
methodsinsteadtreatexposureandpersistentmarketstateas
controlobjects(MoreiraandMuir2017;Harveyetal.2018;
Hamilton 1989). SwingGuard builds on this distinction be-
tween a directional thesis and executable exposure.
FinancialLLMscombinedomainresources(Lietal.2023;
Liu et al. 2023a; Xie et al. 2023) with trading-oriented re-
flection and specialized reasoning (Koa et al. 2024; Li et al.
2024;Wangetal.2024;Dingetal.2024).Multi-agentframe-
works add structured roles (Wu et al. 2023b; Hong et al.
2024), debate (Du et al. 2023; Liang et al. 2024), and scal-
able collaboration (Qian et al. 2025). Beyond role special-
ization, recent work on general LLM reasoning increasingly
introduces explicit structure and control into long-horizon
inference. Structure-aware planning organizes intermediate
reasoning around structured state transitions and verifica-
tion (Xiong et al. 2025), multi-level reasoning decomposes
long trajectories into high-level planning and low-level ex-
ecution (Xiong, Payani, and Fekri 2026), and adaptive in-
formation control regulates the extent and granularity of ev-
idence acquisition in search-augmented reasoning (Xiong
etal.2026).Theseapproachesmotivatestructuredreasoning
and controlled information flow, but address general reason-
ing or retrieval rather than financial risk governance. Finan-
cial systems apply role-based and deliberative mechanisms
to research and trading (Yu et al. 2024; Xiao et al. 2024),
whilerecentcryptocurrencyapplicationsemploymulti-agent
reasoning in portfolio allocation and adversarial trading en-
vironments(Luoetal.2025,2026).SwingGuardinsteadad-
dresses the asynchronous case: faster risk updates condition
strategygenerationandgoverntheactiveswingposition.Be-
yond post-hoc attribution and the broader interpretable-ML
literature (Lundberg and Lee 2017; Rudin 2019; Yeo et al.
2023), it links evidence, risk states, constraints, and realized
positions through an auditable strategy–risk interface.

describe the strategy and risk chains and their mapping to
executed exposure.

Conclusion and Future Work
SwingGuard formulates cryptocurrency swing trading as
dual-timescale control: daily multi-agent reasoning forms a
risk-conditionedtarget,whilethefive-minuteRiskController
maintainstheSystemRiskContext,constrainsexposure,and
records the path from evidence to execution. This coordina-
tionletsriskshapeproposalformationandmaintainexposure
compatibility between daily revisions.
Across BTC and ETH, SwingGuard remains profitable
withthehighestSharpeandlowestdrawdownpointestimates
among reported methods. The capped portfolio also earns a
higherreturnthanequal-weightbuy-and-holdatlowerexpo-
sure. Monthly results support cross-asset consistency, while
theeventtraceestablishesauditablestate-to-positionlinkage.
Ablations support both risk pathways, with the largest
deterioration after removing downstream governance. Sup-
porting ablations distinguish this primary risk-governance
contribution from the complementary effects of specialized
analysis and deliberation.
Future work will test broader regimes and recovery effi-
ciency.

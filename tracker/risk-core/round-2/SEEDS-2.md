## How to read this

- **Message ids.** `M1` to `M54` are his messages, using the numbers in `seeds/operator-words-*.md` and `review-2026-11-07/TIMELINE.md`. `L####` (for example L1386) is a line in the main transcript `2928e6ee-7392-4c1c-87c8-23b375749b4f.jsonl`, counted from 0. It is used for messages that never got an M number.
- **Register ids.** `T-01`, `M-01` and so on are register lines, always with a hyphen. So `M-07` is a line in the methodology group, and `M7` is his message.
- **Older ids.** Square brackets such as `[A23]` point to the item with that id in `seeds/SEEDS.md`. "tension 16" points to line T16 in that file's "Changes of mind and tensions" list.
- **Quotes.** Everything inside double quotes is his, copied exactly, dictation errors included. A script checked every quote against the message cited on its line (`D:\Temp\seeds2\check.py`). The words outside quotes are mine.
- **LATER** marks where he changed his mind or moved on. The earlier words stay.
- **also N**, **also A** and so on mark a line that belongs in a second group. Each line sits once, in its main group.
- "I think" from him means exactly that, and his numbers are examples (M49, M50, M51). He asks to be quoted right: "I think, I did not say it is." (M51)

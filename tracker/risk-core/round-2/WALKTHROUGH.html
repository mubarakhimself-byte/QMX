Your questions

Why lots? Lots are the broker's word for how much of one instrument. A rule speaks in money, which means the same on gold and EURUSD. Lots now exist in one place only, the converter.

What is a starting level? It was a fraction of normal size a unit starts at. It's no longer a rule: size can have a modifier, and how it's set is your methodology session's.

A rule you approve, for house money? That meant you'd sign once and size would then rise by itself. Now it's a block you add or remove. It's on in the scalper template because you asked for it, and its value stays open.

Unit stepping down? A unit moves between active, reducing, benched, halted and restore, read from its journal. Brakes choose which. It's configurable, and only your hand lowers the kill switch.

Can a rule close a position? Yes, and you were right: the dictionary has the mechanisms. The real issue was that the code runs only the first per-bar exit. The exit stack fixes that.

Cents per trade? Not decided here. It came from my old assumptions. Capital sharing is now a row, so your plan decides it in the numbers talk.

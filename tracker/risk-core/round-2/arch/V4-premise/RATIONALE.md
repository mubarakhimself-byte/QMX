# V4 rationale: what the flat unit buys and costs, and when to abandon it

Fable, 7 Nov 2026. Under a page.

**What it is, plainly.** After thinking hard, this shape is close to one the funnel already names: fork **F1 shape A, "compiled in plus a thin group"** (`FUNNEL` section 6), which is `E2`'s Way 1 with Way 4's tags and one config. The funnel called Way 1 dead for "mass re-proof" and Way 4 dead because it "contradicts screens he treats as real" (`E2` (b)). The smallest change that revives shape A and makes it better than the others is three things: (1) a group row's money and limits are **facts** units read live, so one edit reaches 200 units with no recompile and his one click; (2) one structural edit over many units is a **plan**, re-tested in a batch (K8) and clicked once, so the two-fingerprint trick that skips the test is not needed; (3) a cross-unit effect is a **sticker**, so no stage, no guard and no pod evaluator sits in the order path. Everything else is the funnel's core as written. The premise it drops is the second runtime object (section 0 of the spine; census in `D:\Temp\v4-premise\census.tsv`).

**What it buys.** One document kind to compile, one fingerprint, one handler in four drivers, one fold, one override story (preset, patch, plan). The developer adds a brake as a block folder and nothing else unless it needs a new fact, and then the page says so. "The unit already has QML... the dictionary has closing mechanisms" (M49) is taken literally: his first form of the pod, "templatizing it inside the unit", is the shape. Four unit origins take one path because there is no pod reference to resolve. Every pod question is a field: a halt binding, a sticker kind, a group limit. A reckless setup is a preset. The screen keeps "pod" (a group plus its preset), the size trail (the decision row) and head to head (two presets on one logic, compared in QMB). It strikes the funnel's largest row (S14), the group stage, the placement fields and I6 as a kind: about a fifth of the core list, with no row added except the facts registry and the group kind.

**What it costs.** A structural change to many live units is 200 G records per edit, batched; the funnel's shape B avoids that by not re-testing the logic fingerprint, and if G proves slow at 200 units this shape pays in wall time where B pays in trust. A member edited after its preset was painted drifts from its group silently until a fleet report over pins says so; a pod document would have made that drift impossible. "A pod owns the method and a unit in a pod takes its stack" becomes "the preset was applied; the unit owns it; the group's limits warn". Renata's objection stands: a unit that can override its group is not governed, only limited by facts and warned. Account- and group-scope rules cannot be proven in QMB alone (one unit per run); the Lab's multi-unit replay is content. The Lab's seven-slot Pods bench and `PodCompilerPort` to Book and BMS are retired as designs, which he must go through as an overhaul.

**What would make me abandon it.** If a cross-unit rule is needed that must decide per order atomically across units and cannot be written as a fact plus a halt plus a sticker (a true allocator in the order path), the pod as a runtime object returns, and shape B is the honest form. If G at 200 units costs hours and he edits presets daily, the two-fingerprint seat is worth its machine. If he says the pod must govern, not warn, then overlays at compile (reserved field, Deferred) are the next smallest step, not a pod evaluator.

**Where another version is simply better.** Shape B (the funnel's Way 5) is better the day a pod must span PC and server with atomic caps, or the day an edit must reach 200 live units with no test at all. Candidate 3's fact kernel is this spine's facts registry with a better name for its two adapters; I took it. Candidate 1's `safe_region` fallback for a stale-input sizer is a good block and belongs in the gallery. Candidate 2's "a base is a derivation producing dollars; the sizer block only converts" is AD-7's parent. The funnel's walkthrough answers to his M49 questions stand unchanged.

**Honest size.** 32 PRs in the band 30 to 38, 28 before his demo sign-in. Slice 3 (groups, presets, plans) is the only new idea; slices 0 to 2 are the swap chart's WIDEN and WIRE rows under any shape.

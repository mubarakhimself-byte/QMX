VERDICT: REPAIR

## 1. What this part is for

QMB is the tester: it replays a unit over past data with fills, costs, carry and margin that behave like a market, and answers in money and R. The judge is the referee kept outside the tester: it counts every try, pulls the luckiest market back toward the unit's whole result, and gives a verdict in plain words, and the host's one lifecycle writer turns that verdict into draft to tested or draft to parked. The conformance pack proves a tester before it may become the default, and the other run kinds (sweep, walk-forward, robustness, optimize, what-if, replay check) are counted jobs. Data cards record each source's clock and seams, and imports check stamps. The referee and the rules are mostly right. The main weakness is the last connection: several things proved in tests have no path from the real product to them.

## 5. What is fine

- **Gap fills.** A weekend gap records -1.7R, and the literal is checkable by hand: (1.07643 - 1.08510) x 10,000 = -86.70 on 51.00 risked. A stop inside the bar is -1R. The loss side is checked first.
- **Lot rounding.** 0.037 trades 0.03 with 0.1892R residue journaled. 0.004 skips with its reason.
- **Carry mechanics.** Wednesday triple in two zones, three perp fundings at venue times, index financing, dividends, liquidation at the record's level.
- **Holes.** A three-day hole lands `failed` and runs with `allow_gaps`, through the real job. The calendars read correctly, and the priority reads below normal.
- **Bars.** Daily closes follow New York's day through daylight saving. A UTC daily bar that crosses the venue day is refused rather than closed early.
- **The windowed loop.** Flat in memory, no step cap.
- **The judge's rules.** Zero trades never pass, a `not_run` step is never a pass, an unopened run is refused, the same result or an overlapping rerun is refused. 51 variants cluster to 11. The deflated Sharpe formula matches Bailey and Lopez de Prado. A spec may only raise the cluster threshold, which is the safe direction. `fold` refuses the judge moving to paper.
- **Counting.** A sweep, walk-forward or optimize run lands one try per cell, fold or candidate, a failed cell included. Chain evidence is refused from any other run kind. Conservative counting (a sweep also counts its own run) errs against the unit, which is the right way to err.
- **Statistics.** CSCV PBO matches the paper's rank rule. Walk-forward folds never overlap out of sample. Random entries use a `(k+1)/(n+1)` p, so few draws cannot fake significance.
- **Import stamps.** Unit by magnitude, one conversion, seams at file and month boundaries, the clock ledger, and an empty hour held until a second source agrees.
- **Boundaries.** QMB reads only its run folder, never opens the Library, never mints a run id. The `dataset` card v2 is additive and v1 still reads.
- **Replay diff.** Two decisions of one instant, market and side pair in order and never overwrite each other.

Standards axis: no hard breach. Duplicated loops across the sweep, optimize and walk-forward jobs are under the 30-line bar (a judgement call).

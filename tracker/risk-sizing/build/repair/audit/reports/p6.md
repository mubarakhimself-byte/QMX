## 1. What this part is for

Between sessions, workflow templates do the slow work of a trading desk. "Build and prove" turns one sentence into a tested unit and either a paper proposal or a death certificate. The slow lane reads journals and the MIS's published readings each day and writes notes and proposal cards. The node watcher reads heartbeats and node journal rows and says when a node is unwell. Scripts make every number, a model only explains them, a grader holds each number to the step that made it, and nothing here moves a unit, a ref on main or an order. The part also makes every example and first-party app start in CI, and adds a small scanner exemption.

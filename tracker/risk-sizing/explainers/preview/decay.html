Fast stops, slow checks

Loss points protect today. Statistics confirm a dying edge later. Keep the two apart.

  • proposedFast: daily loss, loss points per session, kill switch, on the node
  • proposedSlow: one nightly job per unit journal, four readings
  • proposedStates, not scores: OK, Watch, Alarm

How long a slow check needs

Trades until the edge alarm fires, for an edge that starts at +0.1R per trade.

drops to 0~240
drops to −0.1R~115
drops to −0.3R~55

An earlier file said 1,400 for the first row; the newer check corrected it to about 240. Either way it is slow, so fast protection stays a set of rules.

The four readings

Edge alarm
CUSUM on R

Adds up shortfall vs the baseline. About 1 false alarm per 1,000 trades.

Cone
Below the 5th percentile

Cumulative live R vs the stress-test cone.

Cost drift
> 1.5× card cost

Over the last 50 trades.

Behaviour
Trade rate 0.5×–2×

And replay match above 80%.

Watch: edge alarm past 70% of its limit, or any one other reading. Alarm: edge alarm fires, or two others. All configurable.

QMX unit system

A picture tour of the converged design. Each unit is one complete trading algorithm; everything else only tests it, places it or stops it.

The guiding line

When making strategies is nearly free, judging them is the product.

QMX is your own platform, so effort goes into honest judging and simple machinery.

Markets and gaps

Every market differs in four places: how costs arrive, how time works, what data exists, and how the contract ends. Today the backend models mostly FX.

  • foundStock CFDs at IC are MT5 only; cTrader can't trade them
  • foundIC futures CFDs expire 2 working days early and are never rolled
  • foundcTrader has a depth book but no trade tape
  • foundCCXT is spot only: no perps, no funding, no open interest
  • foundMissing: funding, expiry/roll, dividends, calendars, order-flow indicators

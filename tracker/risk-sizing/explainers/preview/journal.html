The journal

One journal per unit, from creation to retirement. Each part has exactly one writer.

  • proposedBacktest + stress: written by QMB only
  • proposedPaper + live: written by the node only
  • proposedFront page: computed, never typed
  • proposedExam baseline: frozen at promotion, never refitted on live data
  • proposedReaders keep no copy: registry, router, your page, calibrator

08 · Router proposed

The router

The router only routes: it switches units on and off. Speed comes from doing the slow work early, not from clever code.

  • proposedPlanner on the PC writes a signed plan with an expiry
  • proposedGate on the node: cheap checks, only blocks new entries
  • proposedOpen trades always finish under the unit's own exits
  • proposedKill switch is local and independent of the plan
  • openSplit the router: planner on the PC, gate on the node (question 7)

Today: no router at all

The MIS today

6 rule labelers→+ 1 fitted model→read by the Book door and kill switch

Inside the node. No router exists in docs or code. Units' rules never read the MIS, and that stays.

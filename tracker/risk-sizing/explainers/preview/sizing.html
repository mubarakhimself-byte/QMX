Risk and sizing

Each unit sizes itself from its own journal. It shrinks fast, grows back only on fresh evidence, and never passes your ceiling.

  • proposedShrink fast on rules after losses
  • openGrow back only on fresh trades, after a minimum time, inside your ranges (question 4)
  • proposedOne-way ratchet on the account floor, not on size
  • proposedSkip a trade the minimum lot can't size safely, and log it
  • openHouse money only where the record shows streaks (question 5)

Why size must be able to grow back

Measured edges wobble. If size can only go down, every downward wobble sticks, and every unit sits at the minimum within a year.

Shrink only→wobbles stick→all units at minimum

Kelly is a ceiling, not a target

Chance of touching a kill line 40% below the start

Full Kelly~60%
Half Kelly~22%
Quarter Kelly~3%

With a perfectly known edge. Real edges are estimates, so real risk is higher.

Testing in QMB

QMB is the backtest and stress world. It has a lot of machinery, but nothing it produces counts as proof yet.

  • foundAgents can't backtest a strategy they wrote: the door is setup-only
  • foundNo backtest that trades: the only runner is silent
  • foundQuotes only: no crypto, no perps replay
  • foundNo R, no pass marks, no tries correction
  • foundCost calibration empty, so no verdict is proof
  • foundLong scalper tests choke: about 16 GB per month of 1m data (estimate)

The small recommended set

Few tests, each known to catch overfitting, cheap to expensive, stop at the first failure. QMB measures; it never benches or promotes.

Always
Tries ledger

Every run counts. Headline = best of N, deflated.

T1
Cost × 2

Net > 0 at 1× and 2× calibrated cost.

T2
Out-of-sample in R

≥ +0.15R, ≥ 200 test trades, sealed months > 0.

T3
Plateau

Median of nearby settings > 0 after cost.

T4
Family check

PBO ≤ 0.25 passes, > 0.50 dead. From 10 tries.

T5
Size cone

Block-bootstrap: sets size and the live cone. Not a gate.

All numbers are configurable starting values. Optional: 5–10 real event days (news, gaps, spread blowouts). Monthly: a "test of the tests" drops any test that adds no lift.

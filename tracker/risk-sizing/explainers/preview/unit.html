01 · Unit proposed

The unit

One complete trading algorithm. It replaces the trading bot, and it is tested and traded as one piece.

  • proposedEntry and exits live inside the unit
  • proposedSize calibrates itself from its own journal
  • proposedNeeds: the conditions the router must see
  • proposedSession profile, learned in stress and paper
  • proposedExam baseline: what live is judged against
  • proposedOne journal per unit

What one unit can do

Trade
Its own way

Entry, exits and safety exits ship as one.

Size
Itself

Shrinks fast after losses, grows back on fresh evidence.

Time
Pick its sessions

Weights sessions, e.g. Tokyo 70 / London 20 / NY 10.

Markets
State its needs

Spread, volatility and news limits it needs before it fits.

Trust
Judge itself

Live behaviour vs its own exam, not one number for every bot.

Memory
One journal

Backtest, stress, paper and live in one place.

The model it copies

MT5 Wizard: three modules, one EA

Signal→Trailing→Money→One EAships as one

QMX difference: the money part calibrates itself from the unit's journal.

Outside the unit: only three things

1 · Account guard
Stops. Never resizes.

Kill line, daily budget, news windows, kill switch, venue rules.

2 · Pod
Shares one budget

A group of units. Limits units that are really the same bet.

3 · Router
Switches on and off

Which units run where and when. Never how they trade.

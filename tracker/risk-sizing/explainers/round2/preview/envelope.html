The admission envelope

Your click admits a unit together with its markets, sessions and brokers. Inside that envelope the router only directs. Outside it, an offer becomes a paper sibling and a proposal for you.

  • proposedYour click is the only thing that admits a unit to live
  • proposedInside: the router directs; the unit's own conditions decide
  • proposedOutside: never live; a paper sibling plus a proposal card
  • proposedThe home market is never the router's to give or take
  • openThe three 'Router off' buttons are the options of question 3

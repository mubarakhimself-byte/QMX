What changed since round 1

The router directs and never switchesRound 1 had it turning units on and off. Now it only offers, and offers expire.
Units stand aloneRouter, PC link or Library down: a unit keeps trading on its own conditions.
Only you promoteNo automatic minimum-lot promotion. The only automatic step is a risk stop, logged.
Unbounded by defaultNo lookback cap; post-COVID is a default. Plain Python, ML and mini apps are never blocked.
Local firstEverything works on your PC alone. Servers extend or stand in, never replace.

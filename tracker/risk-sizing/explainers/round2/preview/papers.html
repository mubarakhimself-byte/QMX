Two lessons for judging

Lesson 1
Search makes overfitting

The test gap widens with refinement depth; gains fade after about 15 rounds. Count every screen as a try.

Lesson 2
LLM memory is look-ahead

A model may remember what worked. LLM-built units need a test window after the model's training cutoff.

Ten checks for the judging skill

Each is a script. The model explains; numbers decide nothing alone; you decide.

  • proposedJ1 Tries include screens, refinements, mutations and best-of-N picks
  • proposedJ2 Show each winner's round and depth; flag late, deep winners
  • proposedJ3 Overfit signs by count: free settings, blocks, changes, in/out gap, plateau width
  • proposedJ4 Originality and same-bet: two numbers, neither a gate

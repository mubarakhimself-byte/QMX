Round 2 · 02 · Router proposed

The router directs

Like a load balancer: the market is the traffic, units are the servers. The router offers; units decide. It never switches a unit on or off.

  • proposedIts whole door can be one call: offers now, each with an expiry
  • proposedUnits pull offers. Nothing ever waits on the router
  • proposedDecision log: offers issued with their inputs; units log taken or ignored
  • proposedCorrelation ledger: units' results and signals, instruments, long/short consensus
  • proposedDeletion test: remove the router and units lose only extra traffic

Three shapes for the router

AAdvice boardOne central publisher; units pull their offers. Router down: offers expire, units trade their envelope. Keeps a central view of herding and of the router's own value.
BRound 1's planner, gate turned into a lookupSmallest change from what you read. The lookup answers advised, not advised or unknown. Risk: someone wires 'not advised' back into a block.
CEach unit choosesUnits pick from a shared feed of market labels. Nothing central can fail, but there is no central view and the decision log scatters over 200 journals.

One router or many

Shape 1
One planner, slices

Computes offers for all units; each node gets its slice.

Shape 2
One per node

Each node routes its own units; all read one correlation ledger.

Shape 3
Edge + centre

Fast offers per node, slow ones (new markets, siblings) from one planner.

On the PC alone, all three collapse to one router. Adding a node moves only a slice of instruments.

Data and lookback

The lookback limit you heard about is a memory bug in QMB's replay reader. No other cap exists. What really limits '1990' is how far back the data goes.

  • foundRuns are capped at 1,000 steps: about 17 hours of 1-minute bars
  • foundPrices are copied three times per step, and the replay check compares whole frames: four places
  • foundThe docs already say keep all history forever
  • foundThe 12-month seal hides the newest year. It isn't a lookback cap

Three ways to fix the reader

Technical. A likely order: A first, B later.

AOne shared series, a pointer per stepSame answers in linear memory. No unit changes. Unblocks 1990 first.
BKeep only the declared windowConstant memory, like live. Units declare their window; the library already works it out.
CFrames made only when askedLong runs stream, but the biggest change to the contract.

Round 2 · 05 · Journals proposed

Journals and logs

The unit journal is the unit's DevOps layer: monitoring, logging, observability. Beside it, three more streams. Each has one writer; they join by keys, never by copies.

  • proposedUnit journal: the unit's own trades, signals, fills and notes
  • proposedMarket journal: spread, fees, swap and sessions per market, once
  • proposedRouter decision log: every offer with its inputs, replayable
  • proposedGuard journal: so automatic stops are monitored

What the unit journal keeps

Taken
Every trade

Orders, fills, its own slippage, exits.

Seen, not taken
With a reason

Spread, session, max positions, guard, low strength, no offer.

Missed
Found afterwards

Would have fired, but offline or the feed was stale. Found by replay.

Calibration receipts
Which number moved

From which data, by which rule, inside which range.

Offer outcomes
Taken · ignored · expired

Lets us measure whether the router earns its keep.

Agent notes
Notes and proposals only

With evidence links and an expiry, written through a hook.

Three ways to store it

Technical, not your call.

APlain append-only filesMonthly partitions, one writer each. Simplest on the PC.
BObservability tracesEach trade is a trace. The node already ships this monitoring stack; an exporter still writes the files.
CInside the LibraryLineage, search and cards come free, but the catalogue grows with every trade.

A likely blend: A as the record, B for live dashboards, C for notes, proposals and calibration receipts.

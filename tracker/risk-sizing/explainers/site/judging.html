Judging fairly

When strategies are nearly free to make, judging them is the product. Five simple rules.

  • proposed1. Count every try in a family
  • proposed2. Correct the best for that count
  • proposed3. Sealed months the search never saw
  • proposed4. Stress the costs
  • proposed5. Judge live against the unit's own exam baseline

Near-copies count less

Effective tries

10 near-copiesEURUSD 1m, 5m, … of one idea→≈ 2–3 real tries

Sealed months

2021–2024search and tune→2025 · lockedjudged once, like an unseen exam

Enough trades to believe it

55% win, 1:1~270
70% win, small wins~550

Scalpers get there in weeks. A unit that can't get there in time is "too thin to judge", not "failed".

Trust grows with its own record

Weight of its own record = trades ÷ (trades + 100)

20 trades17%
100 trades50%
300 trades75%

A new unit starts near its template's usual size. k = 100 is a configurable start.

Expanding a family

  • proposedEach family has a small false-alarm budget; each new variant spends a little
  • proposedExpand only while: budget left, enough trades in time, neighbours profit, not a copy
  • proposedThe expansion workflow waits until QMX is live

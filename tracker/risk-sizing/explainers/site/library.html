QML, upgraded in place into the library agents use to build units. One small door, a lot behind it.

Two files

Unit spec
What it is

id, version, family, parent, market, logic and settings with ranges, exits, risk template and ranges, sessions, needs, pod.

Journal
What happened

front page, exam baseline, backtest, stress, paper, live, events, notes.

Promises the library keeps

After compiling→Logic and exits never changeonly calibrated numbers move, inside ranges
test()→Only way into backtest/stressthe node is the only writer of paper/live
Every test→Counts as a tryin the unit's family
Inside a hook→No clock, no I/O, no future baronly closed, declared data

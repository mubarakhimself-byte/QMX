When is it "new"?

Logic or an exit changes→New versionlinked to its parent
A setting moves inside its range→Same uniteach value tried = +1 try
The unit recalibrates its risk→Journal entrynot a new unit
A model rewrites it→New unitmarked 'rewritten from', same family

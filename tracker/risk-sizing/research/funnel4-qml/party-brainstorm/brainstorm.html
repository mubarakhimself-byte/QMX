if First Principles Thinking (domain: the if-else and self-tuning question)

  1. B001if A rule is five atoms: a reading (what is measured), a comparison (against what), a threshold (how much), a gate (when the comparison counts: bar close, session, after an arming event) and an action (enter, veto, exit, hand a value to the risk socket). Self-tuning can be stated per atom.
    Seed: his if-else framing T2:779, R36 6.3
  2. B002if Only thresholds and a reading's settings are 'numbers' in his sense ('It's numbers', T2:779); comparison, gate and action are structure. A tuning label per atom tells QML which parts move without a new version.
    Seed: R34 5, glossary calibration D2
  3. B003if Every number carries a motion label beside its origin label: fixed, scaled by a live statistic, refit on a window, chosen per measured state, learned. Origin labels exist since August (source-stated, published-convention...); motion labels do not.
    Seed: R36 q8, SC labels
  4. B004if Write thresholds in units that travel: ATR multiples, percentiles of the instrument's own history, z-scores, spreads or ticks. A rule in pips is one instrument's rule.
    Seed: R35 K15, R36 stops in points 84 vs ATR 9
  5. B005if Percentile thresholds as the default agent form ('RSI below its own 10th percentile over N days'): they adjust per instrument and regime with no optimiser, but hide a lookback that is itself a number and a try.
    New
  6. B006if A branch picked by a measured state is still an if-else (17781: trending stop 100, ranging 50, volatile 150). A regime detector is one more reading feeding a gate, so regime switching needs no special machinery.
    Seed: R36 5.3 pattern 3, R39 C3 regime-detector role
  7. B007if Separate 'a number moved inside its range' (calibration, a journal event) from 'a branch, block or exit family changed' (a variant, a counted try).
    Seed: R35 4.2 synthesis, glossary calibration D2, his 'exits become variant, entry become variant' T1:1177
  8. B008if A rule's own record (where winners turn, how far losers go first) can feed its readings' settings, never its branches. Only the room is designed here; any exit or stop maths stays with the risk session.

else: what kept coming back (connections, not choices)

  1. Most seeds collapse onto one frame: a unit is a graph of if-else atoms over dictionary readings; 'self-tune' is a label on each atom (what moves, what drives it, which clock, who authorises). The frame holds MT5 Wizard, freqtrade-style callbacks, regime switching and ML blocks without new machinery (connection, not a choice)
  2. The dictionary's job is his (one vocabulary so cheap agents never invent words); its August shape (three layers, prose fields, FX scope) is GPT's and is a seed. Review-vs-rebuild is open
  3. The trader lens removes questions: K4, K10, K14, K15, K20 answer several questions agents kept asking; what stays open is identity (when a change makes a new unit), counting tries for structure, and by-eye context
  4. Asset classes split into terrain (instrument record), class-only readings (funding, OI, breadth, order flow) and transfer status per entry; data classes cut across all of them and decide what a primitive can be tested on
  5. Exits and sizing only need room in QML: a labelled exit slot, exit family names, a sizing-modifier role and a socket; the content is the risk session's

- (idea) B002 [STD; seed S-ORDER] Style tiers named by their alarm clock, not by trader type: every tick, every bar close, every session open, every day. A unit subscribes to tiers; an intraday unit can take the session tier and the bar tier together.
- (idea) B003 [STD; seed S-SCALP] Lag-to-hold ratio as the one admission number for a reading into a style tier (starting default: lag at most a third of the median hold; a number, editable). Extends R15 idea 44 from per unit to per tier.
- (idea) B004 [STD; seed S-ORDER] A data ladder, rungs 0 to 6 (calendar, OHLC bars, quote ticks, depth, trade tape, derivatives fields, outside data), as the way "the data a market offers" is stated, per instrument at a venue.

- (idea) B012 [STD; seed S-SNIPE] A sniping-window tier: readings that exist only inside named windows (London open, New York open, overlap, fixes), computed per window instance and compared with past instances of the same window.

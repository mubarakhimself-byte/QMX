## The frame most ideas fit (a connection, not a decision)

## Directions worth carrying into the next step

1. **The finder, not the filter.** For units whose rules already state their context, the MIS answers only "where else is this true now" (B023, B093).
2. **Stamped, versioned, as-of history** as the base under lift, almanac, inefficiency hunts, agent scoring and market twins (B089 to B095, B112).
3. **Agents as slow-tier sensors.** They write in the MIS's words with a timestamp before the session, through tools that cannot see the future, and get scored beside the MIS and beside doing nothing (B033 to B041).
4. **Class packs** as the way the MIS grows limbs: a rung table, a calendar, sensors and vocabulary extras per class (B020), with a rung downgrade path for markets that lack the top rungs (B005).
5. **Health on every reading** and a dark-MIS run in every unit's record, so a unit's independence and the MIS's value are both measured (B061 to B064).
6. **Description, not control, for correlation:** dispersion, clusters and herding counts are published; the risk side decides what to do with them (B043 to B047).

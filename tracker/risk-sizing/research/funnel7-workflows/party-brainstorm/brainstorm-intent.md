# Brainstorm intent: workflow templates around units

Headless `bmad-brainstorming` run, 2026-10-30, stance "ideate for me". Source of truth: `.memlog.md` in this folder (174 entries: 152 ideas W001 to W152, 6 insights, technique switches, directions). Not converged, by design. The HTML report was not produced: the skill hands it to a subagent and this run may not spawn one; `workflows-field.md` is the readable output.

**Topic.** QMX workflow templates that find, build, test, judge, expand, watch and analyse units and markets, plus the skills, hooks and scripts they need.

**Goal.** Expand the field from his seeds and the findings. Ideas, options, permutations and questions only.

**Techniques, one batch per domain** (`brain.py list --all`): Lotus Blossom (intake), SCAMPER (build), Devil's Advocate Courtroom and Reverse Brainstorming (test and judge), Morphological Analysis (expand), Failure Analysis and Evolutionary Pressure (fail and rewrite), Cross-Pollination (market analysis), Five Whys and Persona Journey (unit analysis), TRIZ and Trickster's Gambit (agent proof), First Principles (skills, hooks, scripts), Ecosystem Thinking (triggers), The $0 Mandate and Crank the Dial (cost), Job to Be Done (UI and approvals), Three Rounds of Stupid, Cursed Genie and Sci-Fi Artifact (wildcards).

**Insights logged (INFERRED).**
1. Most of his funnel already exists as draft shapes. What is missing is the doors, the event chain between templates and a shared judge. No new agents are needed.
2. The piece every template reuses is a judge block that the workflow runs. The agent does not call it. The skill explains the verdict and the scripts decide.
3. Proof is about who picks the inputs and when the score is computed. A forecast ledger plus receipts covers the analyst families without anyone comparing by hand.
4. The real cost is the weekly limit and his attention. So: scripts first, agents only on flags, crystallise later, and builder tiers set per template.
5. The shipped builder default is the strong tier, the opposite of his cheap-workhorse seed. It is a setting, so the open question is the default, not the machinery.
6. His click is the only approval. With no in-workflow approval step planned, the publish landing plus an event trigger is the chain that can be built.

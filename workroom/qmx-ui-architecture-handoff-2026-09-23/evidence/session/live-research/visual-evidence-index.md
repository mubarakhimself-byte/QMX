## Known evidence caveats

- `minara-asset-copilot-doc.png` is a failed embedded image capture, not a view of the product surface.
- `quantx-builder-results-comparison.png` is explicitly partial; use the separate QuantX captures for the individual product-image references.
- `xynth-alert-description.png` is visibly blurred by the anonymous sign-up gate. Its underlying text was available in the page accessibility tree, but the image itself cannot substantiate readable layout/content.
- The three OpenBB images are frames from a recorded public demo, not live authenticated controls.
- QuantX captures are official product imagery displayed by the public showcase, not interactive editor evidence.
- Prime’s public demo contains a date inconsistency between the matrix header and expanded narrative; preserve that caveat when citing either screenshot.

See `openbb.md`, `xynth-prime.md`, and `quantx-lse-minara.md` for the source/action context, distinctions between observed behavior and vendor claims, and broader limitations.

Design notes and what remains open

The rail and app/local-navigation relationship come from your storyboard. The separate tabbed work area is informed by your Codex screenshots. Library taxonomy and attachment semantics below are proposals.

  • Files, resource references and tool availability use different selection paths. A plugin can supply capabilities; it is not equivalent to a dataset or a mini-app.
  • Referencing a dataset shows an identified snapshot and declared contents. It does not imply the whole dataset is pasted into a prompt.
  • Referencing an app does not merge its conversation or run it. Opening an app, selecting its tools and referring to it are distinct actions.
  • Snapshot-selection rules, cross-app work, plugin lifecycle and real access behavior need later backend reconciliation.
  • Browser, video, diff and terminal tabs remain in scope. This study exercises a document tab and an object-details tab; no live browser or terminal is embedded.
  • The screenshot's conversation navigator is recorded as a separate candidate; hover, jump and bookmark behavior still need direct evidence.
